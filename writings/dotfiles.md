# Fed up with dotfiles tricks

Really, what is so great about `zsh`, `exa` and other _cool_ tools. I admit, I've been swept by the idea once, mingling with my dots all the time, but it can only take you so far.

Like that boasting for `zoxide` with insanely smart and fast navigation... Did you hear of `CDPATH`? Combined with native `fish` features (which is superior shell) gives you superpowers in navigation and else beyond comprehension. Really, try it, type letter or two and arrow right, out of the box.

What I really try to say is that I cherish my minimal config. It's really `fish`, `fd`, `rg`, `neovim`, `lf`, some custom code living in my [dotfiles](https://github.com/bart-jaskulski/dot) and we're good to go. And when I `ssh` into server, I don't miss my fancy setup, just changing suit into `bash`, `find`, `grep`.

Focus on what can speed you up without slowdowns, and chop off unnecessary load ruthlessly.
