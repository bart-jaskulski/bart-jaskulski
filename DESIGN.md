# Design System: Bartek Jaskulski

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Inhabited Field"**

This is a personal place made of writing, work, and the relationships Bartek chooses to draw between them. Its quiet is inhabited, not empty. Computation makes it effortless to enter and explore; paper, ink, type, and rare cobalt make each choice feel authored. A small observation and a major work receive equal respect, though they need not receive equal space.

Design from within the culture imagined in `vision.md`: technology is ordinary, loved, and everywhere. Historical craft is practiced for pleasure and expression, not as a refuge from machines. The place can be warm, curious, playful, and technically extraordinary without announcing that it is set in 2044. It should feel lived in, not like a solemn museum or a costume of the future.

`vision.md` owns the cultural premise; `PRODUCT.md` owns visitors, publishing behavior, accessibility, and technical constraints. `transcript.md` records exploration, not a backlog: Bartek's later corrections outrank earlier AI interpretations. This is approved direction, not a description of the current implementation.

**Key Characteristics:**

- A continuous warm field carries passages and fragments without defaulting to separate cards.
- Expressive asymmetry and deliberate intervals create pace while preserving reading order.
- Carbon ink carries text; rare cobalt draws attention to focus, active state, and authored relationships.
- A small family of authored marks gives identity without making visitors learn a private alphabet.
- Quiet leaves room for pleasure, surprise, and serious depth.

### Vocabulary

These are design roles, not mandatory components or CSS classes.

| Term | Meaning | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Field** | The continuous ground on which content sits. | Unite pages and let individual pieces occupy different amounts of space. |
| **Passage** | Main text that holds attention: an essay, substantial excerpt, or work description. | Give it the strongest reading order, contrast, and measure. |
| **Fragment** | A complete short observation, including ordinary life or an unfinished inquiry. | Give it room to stand alone without inflating it into a case study. |
| **Interval** | Space between thoughts or modes of attention. | Vary it to establish pace and relationships; it is never an emptiness quota. |
| **Margin** | The supporting edge of a passage or fragment. | Hold dates, references, and authored notes; join the reading flow on narrow screens. |
| **Mark** | A small recognizable authored sign. | Express identity, orientation, or meaningful state with discoverable meaning. |
| **Thread** | An explicit relationship between two pieces, established by Bartek. | Keep a normal link; a cobalt trace or marginal note may reveal it. |
| **Seal** | Bartek's personal monogram. | Express authorship, never cryptographic proof or peer attestation. |

**The Authored Relationship Rule.** Similarity never proves causation. A thread appears only when Bartek has established the association; AI suggestions require his approval.

## Colors

Warm unbleached ivory is the field, deep sumi-like carbon is the ink, and a single piercing electric cobalt is the rare current. The existing OKLCH values are implementation candidates, not approved tokens for this future direction. Resolve their exact values against real rendered content during the build.

### Primary

- **Electric Cobalt:** Focus, active state, and authored connections. Its rarity gives it authority. Links and states must remain understandable without color alone.

### Neutral

- **Warm Washi:** The continuous paper ground, with subtle tonal shifts for structure where needed.
- **Carbon Ink:** Strong primary text and comfortably legible supporting text. Hairlines and nonessential marks may recede; older content may not.

**The Rare Current Rule.** Roughly 3% blue describes scarcity and significance, not a pixel quota. Cobalt does not imply that a claim or identity is verified.

## Typography

Display, prose, and notation have distinct jobs. The exact families remain [to be resolved during implementation]. Existing `Marcellus`, `Prata`, and `Cinzel` are display references, not a settled choice; `Literata`, `Source Serif 4`, and system serif are reading candidates; `JetBrains Mono`, `Commit Mono`, and system monospace are notation candidates.

- **Display:** Expressive stroke character for selected identities and headings. It should support warmth as well as authority, without relentless capitals or monumental scale.
- **Body:** Comfortable English and Polish reading across short observations and complete long essays; support code, footnotes, and references. Begin near a 60–68ch measure and validate with real text.
- **Notation:** Dates, references, and genuine technical information. Essential metadata must remain comfortably readable.

**The Reading First Rule.** Density comes from layered information and careful spacing, never microscopic essential text or a decorative wall of type. A short entry may end after two sentences; a long argument may remain whole.

## Layout

Compose through scale, proximity, alignment, and intervals. A fragment and a substantial passage can sit near one another without becoming equivalent cards. The home selects from a growing body of work; the archive provides a dependable index with factual dates, stable URLs, legible older entries, and direct routes into reading. Dedicated reading pages give full text, code, and references a clear hierarchy. Work can have different spatial weight according to its content, while its factual description follows `PRODUCT.md`.

On narrow screens, recompose asymmetry into a deliberate linear sequence. Keep passages before their supporting margins in reading order and place annotations near the text they explain. No composition requires horizontal scrolling to read content. The first viewport, section sequence, and selected work hierarchy belong to the homepage surface brief, not this global system.

**The Purposeful Interval Rule.** Open space must make content or a relationship more present. It must never postpone the first encounter with real content merely to produce a dramatic hero.

## Elevation & Depth

The field is matte and nearly flat. Fine, visible grain gives the paper character; tonal shifts and overlap may establish depth when they clarify structure. Floating glass panels, artificial distress, long scratches, and shadows used merely to manufacture layers break the material language. Grain must not roughen letters or claim the optical properties of physical washi. Exact texture and depth values remain [to be resolved during implementation].

## Shapes

The recurring silhouette is the relationship between text blocks, open field, hairlines, and authored marks. Use a line where it clarifies a connection or boundary, not around every item. A personal seal should have recognizable stroke character; small custom marks should form a consistent family. Corner radii and stroke measurements remain [to be resolved during implementation]. Decorative kanji, invented coordinates, fake telemetry, and futuristic status codes turn the premise into costume.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** let truthful content determine scale and space; change a composition before inventing copy to fill it.
- **Do** keep Work, Writing, professional history, Contact, and older entries directly reachable through ordinary links and plain labels.
- **Do** make brief interaction feedback precise and settled while keeping text available immediately. Reduced motion gets immediate state changes.
- **Do** test any cobalt thread with real author-established links and equivalent keyboard and touch access before adopting it as a signature behavior.

### Don't:

- **Don't** fade old writing into illegibility, compress essays into compulsory summaries, or enlarge fragments until they appear more important than they are.
- **Don't** use an unfamiliar glyph as the only label for an essential action.
- **Don't** animate reading text as wet ink, delay access with entrances, or keep the page pulsing.
- **Don't** imply invented achievement, causal links, verification, or a runtime AI feature through a visual effect.
